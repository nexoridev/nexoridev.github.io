Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

NoxoriDev Studio is used only by the account owner. It stores the TikTok access token locally on the owner's computer to upload his own videos. No data of other people is collected, sold or shared.

Data used: TikTok account ID and access token (stored locally) and the videos the owner uploads.

Deletion: revoking the app in TikTok deletes access; local data is deleted by the owner.
